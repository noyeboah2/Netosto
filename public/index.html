    Why sickle cell first?

    Common, misunderstood and important

    Sickle cell disease is one of the most common inherited conditions in the world, and it is especially common in Ghana and across sub-Saharan Africa. Many people carry the sickle gene without knowing it.

    Myths about it still spread, for example that it is contagious or caused by a curse. Clear, accurate science helps families make informed decisions and treat people living with it with understanding.
